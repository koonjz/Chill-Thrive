Recovery Science & Awareness

Evidence-based knowledge to help you recover smarter and safer.

What is Cold Therapy?

Cold therapy, also known as cryotherapy, involves controlled exposure to cold temperatures to reduce inflammation, manage pain, and accelerate muscle recovery. It is widely used in sports medicine, physiotherapy, and wellness recovery programs.

When exposed to cold, blood vessels constrict (vasoconstriction), reducing swelling and tissue damage. As the body warms up, circulation increases, helping flush toxins and deliver oxygen-rich blood.

Science Behind Ice Baths

Ice baths are a form of cold water immersion that activate the parasympathetic nervous system, improving stress tolerance and mental resilience.

  • Reduces delayed onset muscle soreness (DOMS)
  • Improves circulation and metabolic recovery
  • Enhances mental focus and stress regulation

Heat vs Cold Therapy

Heat therapy increases blood flow, relaxes muscles, and improves flexibility. Cold therapy reduces pain, inflammation, and swelling.

Alternating heat and cold (contrast therapy) may improve circulation and speed up recovery. The right choice depends on your recovery goal, injury status, and physical condition.

Who Should Avoid Ice Baths?

Ice baths may not be suitable for everyone. Individuals with the following conditions should seek medical advice before cold exposure:

  • Cardiovascular disease
  • High blood pressure
  • Respiratory disorders
  • Nerve sensitivity or Raynaud’s syndrome

Recovery Myths & Facts

  • Myth: Ice baths are only for professional athletes.
  • Fact: Anyone can benefit with proper guidance.
  • Myth: Longer cold exposure is better.
  • Fact: Optimal duration matters more than intensity.
